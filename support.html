Ciao Cards: Support

Questions, mistakes in the vocabulary, problems with the app: write to ciaocardssupport@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you did before the problem appeared. You get an answer within two working days.

How it works

First start. Choose the language you speak (English, Russian or both), your Italian level and a daily goal. When the app starts you see START; tap it to begin a round.

The card. The front shows a word. Tap to turn the card, then swipe right if you knew it and left if you did not. The round button with the arrow takes back your last swipe.

The number on the card. "New" means you see the word for the first time. "35 d" means the word comes back in 35 days. Words you know well come back rarely; words you miss come back soon.

Daily goal. The goal is the number of cards you want to answer in a day. Words that are due come first; new words fill the rest. When nothing is left, the app says "Done for today". "Practise more" gives you more new words or repeats your weakest words.

Levels. The built-in vocabulary runs from A1 to C2. New words come from your current level. When you have seen all words of a level, the app asks whether you want to start the next level or stay. You can change the starting level and the current level in Settings → Standard Vocabulary.

A word you already know. Press and hold the card and choose "I know this word". It will not be asked again. You can undo this in Settings → Standard Vocabulary → Browse.

Two cards with the same translation. Some Italian words mean the same in English or Russian. The card then tells you which word is not wanted, for example "now (not: adesso)".

Verbs. Turn a verb card and tap Forms to see the present, the passato prossimo and the future.

Sound

  • The back of the card is read aloud. Tap the speaker to hear it again; tap once more to hear it slowly. Tap the example sentence to hear the sentence.
  • Better voices are free. Open the Settings app of your iPhone, search for "Voices", choose Italian and download a voice marked Premium or Enhanced. Then choose it in Ciao Cards under Settings → Sound → Voices.
  • No sound? Check that Sound is on in the app's Settings and turn the volume up.

Your own words

  • Settings → Modify words or Modify expressions → +. Type the word in one language; the other languages are filled in automatically and you can correct them.
  • Automatic translation needs the language to be installed on the iPhone. iOS asks you once whether it may download it.
  • "Suggest example" appears only on iPhones with Apple Intelligence.
  • Notion. If you keep vocabulary in a Notion table, Settings → Update from Notion reads it. The screen explains how to create a Notion token. The app only reads; it never changes your Notion pages.

Backup and a new iPhone

  • Any list → … → Export backup saves one file with your words, your progress and your settings. Keep it in Files or iCloud Drive.
  • On a new iPhone install the app and choose … → Restore backup.
