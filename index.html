Ciao Cards

Italian by level, one card at a time.

Ciao Cards is a flashcard trainer for the iPhone. It contains more than 13,000 Italian words, verbs and expressions from level A1 to C2 with English and Russian translations, example sentences and verb forms. The app remembers how well you know every word and brings it back shortly before you would forget it. You can also add your own words and expressions.

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
