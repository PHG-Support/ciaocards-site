Ciao Cards: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

In short: Ciao Cards does not collect any data about you. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see your words, your progress or anything else you do in the app.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • the words and expressions you add yourself;
  • your learning progress: for every card when you answered and how well, and which words you marked as known;
  • your settings, for example the languages, the level and the daily goal;
  • if you connect Notion: your Notion token, kept in the iPhone's Keychain;
  • one small technical note in the Keychain: the number of the app build you installed first.

All of this stays on your iPhone. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Like other app data, it can be part of the backups of your iPhone that you make yourself with iCloud or a computer.

When something leaves your iPhone

Only when you ask for it:

  • Notion (optional). If you connect your own Notion workspace and tap an update, the app asks Notion's service (api.notion.com) for the pages you chose and reads your vocabulary tables from them. Your token is sent to Notion only. The app writes nothing to Notion. Notion's own privacy policy applies to what Notion does.
  • Backup files. A backup file is created only when you tap Export. You decide where it goes. It contains your words, your progress and some settings. It never contains your Notion token.

What happens on the iPhone and is not sent anywhere

  • Translation of your own words uses Apple's translation on the device. iOS may ask you whether it may download a language from Apple.
  • Example suggestions use Apple Intelligence on the device, on iPhones that have it.
  • Speech uses the voices installed on your iPhone.
  • The built-in vocabulary is part of the app and needs no connection.

Crash reports

The app contains no reporting code of its own and no code from other companies. If you have allowed your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives the developer crash reports and usage numbers that do not identify you. If you test the app through TestFlight and send feedback, Apple passes on what you send.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Notion in the app to remove the token from your iPhone.
  • Reset statistics in the app to delete your progress.
  • Delete the app to delete everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published on this page with a new date.
